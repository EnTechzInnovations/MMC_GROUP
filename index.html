Our Story

A journey of four decades, built on passion, persistence and the unwavering vision of one man from Triprayar.

ABOUT US

Our journey began four decades ago in the small village of Triprayar, located in the Thrissur District of Kerala. The growth we have achieved today is the result of the vision, ambition, and strong willpower of our CMD, Mr. Sainul Abdeen Shah. During the early years, Mr. Sainul Abdeen Shah travelled across the length and breadth of the state, gradually gaining momentum by securing numerous OOH campaigns. Over time, he recognised the opportunity to provide comprehensive one-stop solutions to our esteemed clients, which led to the establishment of Meridian International OOH Media Solutions, Optima Digital Printing Solutions, Mode Media Communications, and Atees Engineering Industries.

"I personally thank all my esteemed clients as well as my employees for their unconditional support for our mutual growth year after year."
— Mr. Sainul Abdeen Shah, CMD

The MMC Group Story

OUR STORY — From a Single Vision in 1985 to a Four-Division Powerhouse

Our story was written in 1985 by our ever-energetic and positive visionary CMD, Mr. Sainul Abdeen Shah. Within just over ten years of Mode Media Communications, we launched Meridian International OOH Media Solutions. Two years later, we started our CMD's dream project — Optima Digital Printing Solutions. Within another year, Atees Engineering Industries was born. In just 10–15 years, we diversified into 3 divisions and formed a group company — MMC Group.

1985 — Mode Media Communications founded
1995 — Meridian International OOH launched
1997 — Optima Digital Printing launched
1998 — Atees Engineering Industries launched
2000 — MMC Group, a complete branding powerhouse
