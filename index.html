Our Story

A journey of four decades, built on passion, persistence and the unwavering vision of one man from Triprayar.

OUR JOURNEY

Debuted four decades ago from the small village of Triprayar in Thrissur District, Kerala, the growth we achieved today is the result of the ambitious and strong will power of our CMD, Mr. Sainul Abdeen Shah. During those initial days, Mr. Sainul Abdeen Shah travelled across the length and breadth of the state, slowly gaining momentum in terms of getting innumerable OOH Campaigns. Over a period of time, he realised there was an opportunity to provide one-stop solutions to our esteemed clients — leading to the formation of Meridian International OOH Media Solutions, Optima Digital Printing Solutions, Mode Media Communications and Atees Engineering Industries.

"I personally thank all my esteemed clients as well as my employees for their unconditional support for our mutual growth year after year."
— Mr. Sainul Abdeen Shah, CMD

OUR HERITAGE

Born to a family with humble background in Valappad, Triprayar in Thrissur District, Mr. Sainul Abdeen Shah has been a great admirer of his late father, Mr. A.T. Kunjhimon. His father was mainly into agriculture and influenced him greatly in terms of dreaming big and achieving those dreams.

"I owe all my success and achievement to my father."
— Mr. Sainul Abdeen Shah, CMD

LEGACY

Being a self-starter with positive energy, our CMD himself is the source of immense positive energy to achieve greater results, ensuring TEAM WORK along with sincerity and commitment. This is the legacy he imbibed from his father and continues to do so.

GROUP PURPOSE

Our prime intention is to provide turnkey solutions related to Branding and scripting greater milestones for our clients — avoiding multiple vendor contacts for various relevant promotional activities. We are a true one-stop branding partner.

MILESTONE

We have been instrumental and exclusive in associating with top brands in Kerala and South India during their launches — which later became market leaders. We also created awareness messages during the Covid period displaying safety precautions as advised by the Health Department.

VISION & VALUES

To be at the forefront in providing the best options to our clients and timely execution of those projects to the utmost satisfaction of our ongoing as well as forthcoming clients.

Setting values to each of our projects — small or big — with maximum professionalism and time commitment, and enhancing their brand value.

LEADERSHIP

To meet visionaries of the highest caliber, mutual exchange of ideas with decision makers of various companies, and put those ideas into practice to achieve even greater goals. Our leadership philosophy is rooted in collaboration, continuous learning and a relentless drive toward excellence.

The MMC Group Story

From a Single Vision in 1985 to a Four-Division Powerhouse

Our story was written in 1985 by our ever-energetic and positive visionary CMD, Mr. Sainul Abdeen Shah. Within just over ten years of Mode Media Communications, we launched Meridian International OOH Media Solutions. Two years later, we started our CMD's dream project — Optima Digital Printing Solutions. Within another year, Atees Engineering Solutions was born. In just 10–15 years, we diversified into 3 divisions and formed a group company — MMC Group.
